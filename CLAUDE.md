## Project purpose

Per `README.md` (in German): this is a "Sprachassistent für Senioren" (voice assistant for seniors). The repository is meant to hold a complete software-engineering example built with AI agents over the course of the winter semester 2026/27. The goal is to realize a full process — from problem identification through to IT operations — with the help of an AI agent.
